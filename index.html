Realiscon, 2026. Project role: LEED consultant

LEED v5 strategy for a heritage hotel

A locally protected former sanatorium, planned as a hotel. The project targets LEED v5 BD+C: New Construction and Major Renovations.

Status: strategy stage. The project has not been certified.

Consulting scope
Compiled the optional add-on studies and calculations for the company's LEED consulting proposal and supported its pricing.
Client presentation
Presented the proposed strategy to the client.

Selected design work

Master's thesis, BME, 2022

Housing for intergenerational living

Social rental housing in CLT, built around a shared courtyard garden, designed for residents of different generations. A covered gallery and a roof that opens in warm weather were intended to help air move through the courtyard across the seasons.

What this project shows

A program-first approach to housing need: modest means, shared space at the center, and timber construction. The climate strategies are design intent, not measured performance.

Academic studio and course work, ENSA Montpellier and BME, 2020

CLT school

A school in cross-laminated timber for a hypothetical site in Amsterdam. I began it in a design studio during my Erasmus semester in France, then developed its construction details in a building construction course at BME the following semester.

What this project shows

Taking a timber concept from design intent to buildable detail, across two schools and two languages.

University workshop with a fellow student, 2020

Benches from leftover materials

At a university workshop hosted by a prefabricated tiny-house manufacturer, a fellow student and I designed and built two benches from the factory's leftover materials, including high-pressure laminate (HPL). One of them has a small sliding table.

What this project shows

Hands-on design-build work and designing around the materials available.
